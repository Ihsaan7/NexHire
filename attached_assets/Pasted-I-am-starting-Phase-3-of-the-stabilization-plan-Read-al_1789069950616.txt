3A. SAVE PRACTICE SESSIONS TO DATABASE

    userId (required)
    mode (required) — "cv" | "job" | "custom"
    topic (String) — job title or custom topic if applicable
    questions (Array) — each item: { question, userAnswer, 
      aiFeedback, score }
    totalScore (Number) — average across all questions
    status (String) — "incomplete" | "complete"

    updatedAt (Date)
